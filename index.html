Welcome

This is a faster, paperless way to check in at RHU Carmen. Answer a short set of questions about your health, symptoms, and history before your visit, so our staff can prepare and attend to you more quickly.

Carmen, Cebu Secure & Confidential Takes About 5 Minutes

Before You Begin

A quick heads-up so your check-in goes smoothly.

  • 1Have a valid ID and your PhilHealth number (if available) ready — you'll be asked for these.
  • 2List down any current medications and known allergies beforehand; it speeds up the medical history section.
  • 3Answer honestly and completely — your responses directly guide how staff prioritize and treat your visit.
  • 4Your information stays within RHU Carmen's records and is used only for your care and triage.
  • 5If you're filling this out for someone else (a child, an elderly relative), use their details, not your own.

A Few More Reminders

Some things worth knowing before you get started.

  • 6Regular clinic hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM, except holidays. Walk-ins are welcome, but arriving early helps you get seen sooner.
  • 7After you submit, please wait for your name or number to be called at the waiting area — no need to keep approaching the counter.
  • 8Bring a companion if you need help with mobility, hearing, or reading — staff can also assist you in English or Bisaya/Cebuano.
  • 9Some visits (lab work, referrals, or seeing a specific provider) may take longer than others — thank you for your patience.
  • 10Not sure about something on the form? You can leave it blank and ask the front desk once you arrive.
In an active emergency (severe bleeding, difficulty breathing, unconsciousness), go straight to the RHU front desk or call for help — don't wait to fill out the form first.

Filling This Out for a Child or an Elderly Relative?

This happens often — here's how to do it correctly.

  • 11Fill in the patient's own name and details (the child's, or your elderly relative's) — not the details of whoever is typing the form.
  • 12Note the full name and relationship of the parent, guardian, or relative assisting (e.g. "Mother," "Son," "Caregiver") — front desk staff will ask for this too.
  • 13If the patient is a minor, a parent or legal guardian must sign a Consent to Care form at the RHU front desk before treatment can proceed.
  • 14Bring both IDs if possible — the patient's (or their birth certificate/PhilHealth for a child), and the assisting relative's own valid ID.
  • 15The assisting relative may answer history questions on the patient's behalf, but symptoms and history should reflect the patient, not the person filling out the form.

Getting the Most Out of Your Visit

A few extra pointers from our staff.

  • 16Senior citizens, PWDs, and pregnant patients may use the priority lane at the front desk — let staff know when you arrive.
  • 17The building is wheelchair-accessible with a ramp at the main entrance; feel free to ask staff for assistance getting around.
  • 18Waits can vary depending on the day — bringing water and something to keep you occupied can help if it gets busy.
  • 19Double-check your contact number on the form — it's how staff will reach you if there are updates about your visit.
  • 20You can review and edit your answers before submitting — take your time, there's no rush.
